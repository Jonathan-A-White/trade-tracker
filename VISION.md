## Vision (draft, from his words above)
- An app that tracks what you buy at the grocery store and helps you understand your spending.
- Set a budget at any store and scan items into the cart; staying on budget and adding an unknown item are easy.
- After the trip it helps you see what you could do differently, and it shows how rising prices affect you.
- For him first, and for any shopper; speed in the aisle matters most.
- Later (his 'for the future'): prices shared on chain for other users who choose to use them.
- YNAB (his, 2026-10-29): the app grows into a helper for the family's YNAB, and it fits around YNAB, never reinvents it, leaving room to fit around another budget app later.
- YNAB is a connection, On or Off in Settings, Off for anyone who has not turned it on; while Off nothing of YNAB is drawn anywhere. The Personal Access Token is never kept in the clear: it is sealed under the key on the device.
- Families are isolated from each other as much as possible, with the privacy practices stated plainly; he and his wife are the first users, set up so that others can join without a big lift.
- Lines: local-first and offline, nothing blocks on the network; the app never makes the trip slower; a price the AI guessed is marked for his check.
## How feedback is weighed
Feedback that fits this vision is approved and built elegantly; where people differ, a setting; anything crossing a line or changing the vision goes to the Governor.
